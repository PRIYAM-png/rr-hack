How pressure and scores are calculated

Pressure index (0–1) for every delivery. Chasing: 45% required run rate (absolute and versus current rate), 30% wickets lost, 25% how late in the innings. Setting a total: 45% wickets lost, 35% how late, 20% scoring behind par. Knockout matches add 0.08. Balls above the chosen percentile within each format and innings are “pressure”.

Value per ball. Runs and wickets are compared with the league average for the same format, innings, phase and pressure level, so a player is only credited for beating peers in the same spot. A wicket is worth the chosen number of runs.

Score. 60% pressure value per 100 balls (shrunk toward zero for small samples), 20% change from the player’s own non-pressure value, 20% share of matches with positive pressure impact. Players below the ball and match minimums are not ranked.
